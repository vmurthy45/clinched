    2010 F1 title race, in brief

    Sebastian Vettel won the 2010 Formula 1 World Championship with 256 points, beating Fernando Alonso (252) by 4 points. The title was settled at the final round.

    The biggest lead of the season was 14 points, held by Hamilton after the German Grand Prix. Every result counted towards the championship.

    Questions

    Who won the 2010 F1 World Championship?

    Sebastian Vettel won with 256 points and 5 wins, driving for Red Bull. Fernando Alonso was second on 252.

    How close was the 2010 F1 title race?

    The final margin was 4 points. The lead changed hands 9 times, and 6 drivers led the standings during the season.

    When was the 2010 F1 title decided?

    At round 19 of 19, the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix (14 November 2010).

    Would the 2010 F1 champion change under other points systems?

    No. Vettel wins under all of the alternative systems we test.
