    1990 F1 title race, in brief

    Ayrton Senna won the 1990 Formula 1 World Championship with 78 points, beating Alain Prost (71) by 7 points. The title was settled at the Japanese Grand Prix, with 1 round to spare.

    The biggest lead of the season was 18 points, held by Senna after the Portuguese Grand Prix. Only each driver's best 11 results counted.

    Questions

    Who won the 1990 F1 World Championship?

    Ayrton Senna won with 78 points and 6 wins, driving for McLaren. Alain Prost was second on 71.

    How close was the 1990 F1 title race?

    The final margin was 7 points. The lead changed hands 2 times, and 2 drivers led the standings during the season.

    When was the 1990 F1 title decided?

    At round 15 of 16, the Japanese Grand Prix (21 October 1990).

    Would the 1990 F1 champion change under other points systems?

    No. Senna wins under all of the alternative systems we test.
