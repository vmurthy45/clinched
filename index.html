Sebastian Vettel won the 2010 Formula 1 World Championship with 256 points, beating Fernando Alonso (252) by 4 points. The title was settled at the final round.

Points behind the leader

Solid bar: points now. Hatched: the most that driver could still reach by winning everything left. The black line is the leader's score, and a driver whose reach falls short of it is out.

Every result, top of the table

Number is the finishing place. R: did not finish. Blank: did not take part. Other codes: DNS did not start, DNQ did not qualify, DSQ disqualified, NC not classified.
