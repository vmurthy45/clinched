How it works

Clinched replays every Formula 1 drivers' championship round by round. It is an independent fan project.

Tension score

Each finished season is ranked on four things: how small the final margin was (measured in race wins, so eras compare fairly), how late the title was settled, how often the lead changed, and how much of the year the top two spent within one win. The score out of 100 is the average of those four rankings.

Still alive

After each round we give a driver the maximum from every remaining race, including sprints and fastest-lap points where they existed, and apply that season's dropped-score rule. A tie with the leader counts as alive, because countback could still settle it. For finished seasons the official record has the final say on when a title was won.

Sprint weekends

A sprint and its Grand Prix count as one round, so standings are shown after the whole weekend.

What-if scoring

The alternative systems apply to Grand Prix finishing places only, with no sprints, fastest-lap points or half-points races, and every result counts. Only drivers who scored officially are included, so the lower table is approximate.

Data

Results come from F1DB. As a check, every driver's final total is recalculated from race points under that season's rules and compared with the official table before each update.
